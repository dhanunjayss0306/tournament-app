Login

Log in to your role.

Three roles, each with its own login. Top Admin creates tournaments and announces winners. Admin / Scorer scores matches live. Player registers and watches live scores.

Preview login. Any code works here. In the real app each role has its own account and code.

What each role can do

Top Admin: creates tournaments, sets sport, teams, players, format and entry fee, assigns scorers, announces the winner.

Admin / Scorer: stands at the court or ground and scores live. Plus 1 in badminton, ball by ball in cricket, goals in football.

Player: registers, pays the entry fee, and watches live scores, fixtures and results on their phone. No scoring controls.

Scores, tournaments, fixtures, registrations and winners are saved on this device. Live sync across phones needs a Firebase project, see sync.js.
